Case study 01

Employee Onboarding & Internal HR Portal

The Employee Onboarding & Internal HR Portal is a digital workplace initiative designed to improve the employee experience by centralizing essential organizational information, onboarding resources, internal communications, and HR-related services. The project responds to challenges such as fragmented onboarding instructions, inconsistent employee communication, and limited access to relevant internal resources. Drawing on her experience across employee lifecycle management and HR operations, Mahshid identified key HR needs, structured business requirements, defined workflows, contributed to product documentation, and prepared content and specifications for technical implementation. The solution illustrates how practical HR knowledge can be translated into a structured digital employee experience.

Organizational challenge
Fragmented onboarding guidance and internal information can make it difficult for employees to find the support they need.
HR solution & capabilities
A central employee portal with structured onboarding journeys, role-based resources, internal communications and employee self-service concepts.
Mahshid’s contribution
HR needs analysis, stakeholder interviews and requirements discovery, business planning, PRD preparation, workflow definition, functional and technical handoff specifications, and HR content preparation.
Intended HR value
Intended to make onboarding more consistent, information easier to find and employee communication clearer.

Case study 03

HR Engagement & Events Calendar 1405

The HR Engagement & Events Calendar is an interactive planning resource designed to help People & Culture teams turn important cultural, national, and international occasions into meaningful employee-engagement opportunities. Instead of functioning as a conventional date calendar, it connects relevant occasions with ideas for internal communications, employee recognition, team activities, and organizational culture initiatives. The tool presents a full-year visual overview, detailed monthly listings, occasion categories, and activity suggestions, helping HR teams coordinate engagement efforts throughout the year. This project reflects Mahshid’s approach to combining people-focused planning, structured HR content, and accessible digital tools.

Organizational challenge
Relevant occasions and engagement ideas are often spread across separate calendars and planning documents.
HR solution & capabilities
A Persian RTL calendar for 1405 with an annual map, color-coded national, religious and international occasions, category and type filters, monthly details and interactive tooltips.
Mahshid’s contribution
HR engagement concept, planning requirements, selection and organization of occasions, employee-activity ideas, categorization, content preparation, functional requirements and user experience.
Intended HR value
Intended to help HR teams coordinate recognition, internal communications and workplace culture activities throughout the year.
